Destination

Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic – Kandy

The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic is one of the most sacred Buddhist temples in Sri Lanka. It is located in the beautiful city of Kandy and is famous for housing a tooth relic of Lord Buddha. The temple has beautiful traditional architecture, golden decorations, and great historical value. Many local and foreign visitors come here to worship, learn about Sri Lankan culture, and experience its peaceful atmosphere. It is also recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site as part of the Sacred City of Kandy.

Coconut Tree Hill – Mirissa

Coconut Tree Hill is one of the most beautiful coastal attractions in Mirissa, located in southern Sri Lanka. It is famous for its tall coconut trees growing on a small hill overlooking the blue Indian Ocean. Visitors can enjoy breathtaking sea views, take beautiful photographs, and relax while feeling the fresh ocean breeze. The hill is especially popular among tourists during sunrise and sunset. It is a wonderful place for nature lovers and anyone who wants to experience the beauty of Sri Lanka's southern coast.

ParaviduPatha – Matara

Paravi Du Patha, also known as Pigeon Island, is a small and beautiful island located near Matara in southern Sri Lanka. It is famous for its peaceful surroundings, rocky coastline, and stunning views of the Indian Ocean. The island is home to many pigeons and other birds, making it an interesting place for nature lovers. Visitors can enjoy the beautiful coastal scenery, take photographs, and experience the natural charm of the area. Paravi Du Patha is a unique attraction that adds to the beauty and cultural value of Matara.

Nine Arch Bridge – Ella

The Nine Arch Bridge is one of the most famous attractions in Ella, Sri Lanka. This impressive railway bridge is surrounded by green mountains, tea plantations, and thick tropical forests. It is well known for its beautiful stone arches and unique construction. Visitors often come to watch trains travel across the bridge and take photographs of the stunning scenery. The surrounding environment creates a peaceful and refreshing atmosphere. The Nine Arch Bridge is a must-visit destination for nature lovers, photographers, and travelers exploring Sri Lanka's hill country.

Unawatuna Beach – Galle

Unawatuna Beach is a popular beach destination located near Galle in southern Sri Lanka. It is famous for its golden sandy beach, clear blue water, and beautiful tropical surroundings. Visitors can swim, relax under palm trees, enjoy delicious seafood, and watch colorful sunsets over the ocean. The area also offers opportunities for snorkeling and exploring nearby coastal attractions. With its relaxing atmosphere and natural beauty, Unawatuna is an ideal place for families, couples, and travelers looking for a memorable beach holiday.

Hikkaduwa Beach – Hikkaduwa

Hikkaduwa Beach is a beautiful coastal destination on the southern coast of Sri Lanka. It is famous for its clear ocean water, coral reefs, colorful tropical fish, and lively beach atmosphere. Visitors can enjoy snorkeling, surfing, swimming, and exploring the fascinating marine environment. The area also has many restaurants, cafés, and shops where tourists can experience local food and culture. Hikkaduwa is a wonderful destination for adventure seekers and nature lovers who want to discover the beauty of Sri Lanka's coastline and marine life.
